# Welcome to openSME

This workspace is your self-hosted productivity suite — identity, cloud
storage, mail, groupware, online document editing and workflow automation,
all behind a single sign-on. Everything here runs on infrastructure you
control; no third party sees your files or your mail.

## Your services at a glance

| Service      | What it is                                                    |
|--------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| Identity     | Your single sign-on (Zitadel). One account, every service.    |
| Cloud        | Files, folders and sharing (OpenCloud).                       |
| Webmail      | Mail, calendar and contacts (SOGo / Stalwart).                |
| Office       | Online document editing (Collabora) — open a cloud file, edit in place. |
| Workflow     | Automated processes (Operaton BPMN) — approvals, requests, tasks. |
| Portal       | This landing page — every service one click away.             |

## First steps

1. Sign in through **Identity** — the same account works everywhere.
2. Drop a file into **Cloud** and open it in **Office** to edit online.
3. Find a workflow you need on the **Workflow** service and start an instance.
4. Book your own dashboard the way you like it — the Portal is yours.

## Where to go for help

- **Cloud** holds the shared files; **Webmail** is your mail.
- The **Support** desk for tickets and a knowledge base.
- The machine-readable runbooks in `opensme-knowledge/` for operators.

Seeds like this document live in `bootstrap/seed-content/` — copy or fork
them to make onboarding material that fits your organisation.
