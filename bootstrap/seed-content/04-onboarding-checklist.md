# New-member onboarding checklist

A repeatable list for bringing a new person into openSME. Store a filled
copy in **Cloud → Team → People** and update it as members move through.

## Day one — access

- [ ] Account created in **Identity** (single sign-on, one login granted).
- [ ] Can sign in to the **Portal** and reach every service they need.
- [ ] Shared team folders shared with them in **Cloud**.
- [ ] Mailbox provisioned in **Webmail**; knows the calendar address.

## Week one — working

- [ ] Opened and co-edited a document in **Office** from the **Cloud**.
- [ ] Started and participated in one **Workflow** instance (e.g. a leave
      request or a purchase approval).
- [ ] Posted a meeting note + cloud link as a **Team Note**.
- [ ] Read the **Team Charter** and opened the **Support** ticket flow.

## Week two — operations

- [ ] Knows where **Team → Operations** runbooks live.
- [ ] Knows who to call for tasks vs. tickets.
- [ ] Added to the on-call rotation in the calendar (if applicable).

## Checklist hygiene

Update the checklist in the **Cloud** (not locally) so everyone reads one
version. Delete stale copies to keep the folder honest.
